About

I'm Nirmal Goradara, an independent developer who builds real, working software for e-commerce sellers and growing businesses. My core focus is supply chain analytics: turning a seller's own order, inventory and sales data into information they can act on.

Around that core, I also offer data analytics, ERP solutions, AWS cloud solutions, website building and cyber security, so a business can get its software, hosting and data protection from one person.

I work independently, so clients deal directly with the person who builds and supports the solution.

Services

  • Supply chain analytics for e-commerce sellers, covering inventory, orders and sales performance.
  • Data analytics that turns business data into clear reports and decisions.
  • Business management software that brings a seller's day-to-day data into one place.
  • ERP solutions to manage operations, inventory, orders and finances in one system.
  • Cloud solutions (AWS) for hosting, deploying and scaling business applications.
  • Website building for businesses and online sellers.
  • Cyber security to protect business systems and data.
  • Amazon integrations using the Selling Partner API (SP-API), with access only to data the seller authorizes.
